Data Deletion Instructions

Tale Verse

You can request deletion of your Tale Verse account information and saved reading progress at any time.

How to Request Deletion

  1. Send an email to iverhanji@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject line Tale Verse – Data Deletion Request.
  3. In your message, provide the email address associated with your Tale Verse account and tell us whether you signed in with Facebook or Google. If available, include your Tale Verse user ID.
  4. We may contact you to verify that you control the account before processing the request. Do not send your password.

Email a deletion request

What Will Be Deleted

After we verify your request, we will delete or arrange deletion of the personal information associated with your Tale Verse account that we control, including basic profile information stored for your account and saved story-reading progress.

Processing and Retention

We will process verified requests as soon as reasonably practicable. Some information may be retained where required by law, for legitimate security purposes, or in protected backups until those backups are routinely overwritten. If any information must be retained, it will be limited to what is necessary for that purpose.

Important: Revoke Sign-In Access Separately

Deleting data from Tale Verse does not automatically revoke the App's permissions in your Facebook or Google account. You can also remove Tale Verse from the connected-app or third-party access settings in the relevant provider account. Revoking provider access alone does not delete information already stored by Tale Verse; please send us a deletion request as described above.

Contact

For questions about deletion, contact iverhanji@gmail.com.
