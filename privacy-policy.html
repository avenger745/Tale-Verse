Privacy Policy

Tale Verse

Effective date: November 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Tale Verse ("we," "us," or "our") handles information when you use the Tale Verse application ("App"). Tale Verse is an entertainment application for reading stories and saving your reading progress.

1. Information We Collect

When you sign in using Facebook or Google, we may receive basic profile information that the sign-in provider makes available to the App and that you authorize, such as your name, email address, profile photo, and provider account identifier. The exact information depends on the permissions granted and the information made available by the provider.

We also store information needed to maintain your reading experience, including your reading progress (for example, the stories you read and your position in a story) and information that associates that progress with your account.

Please do not assume that the App collects only the examples listed above if its features or permissions change. We will update this policy if our data practices materially change.

2. How We Use Information

We use the information described above to:

  • create or identify your Tale Verse account;
  • connect your account when you choose Facebook or Google sign-in;
  • save and restore your story-reading progress across sessions or devices; and
  • operate, maintain, and protect the App.

3. Sign-In Providers

If you choose Facebook or Google sign-in, that provider handles the authentication process under its own privacy policy and terms. Tale Verse does not ask you to provide your Facebook or Google password to us. Your use of those services is also subject to the relevant provider's policies:

  • Meta Privacy Policy
  • Google Privacy Policy

4. Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share it with third parties for their independent advertising purposes. Information may be processed by service providers that help us operate the App, such as database or hosting providers, where necessary to provide the service. Such providers should use the information only to provide services to us and protect it appropriately. We may also disclose information when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect the App, its users, or our rights.

5. Data Retention and Security

We retain account information and reading progress while they are needed to provide the App. If you request deletion, we will take reasonable steps to delete the associated information, subject to information we are legally required or permitted to retain. We use reasonable measures intended to protect stored information, but no method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Your Choices and Data Deletion

You may stop using the App and revoke its access through your Facebook or Google account settings. Revoking access does not necessarily delete information already stored by Tale Verse. To request deletion of your Tale Verse account information and associated reading progress, follow the instructions on our Data Deletion page or email iverhanji@gmail.com.

7. Children's Privacy

Tale Verse is an entertainment application. It is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in circumstances where parental consent is required. If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, contact us so we can review the request.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the App or its data practices change. We will post the updated policy at this URL and revise the effective date above.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how Tale Verse handles information, contact us at iverhanji@gmail.com.
